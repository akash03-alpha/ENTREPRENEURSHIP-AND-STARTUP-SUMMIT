Startup Pitch Competition

The Pitch Competition gives students and young entrepreneurs an opportunity to present their innovative business ideas to a panel of judges and investors.

Competition Rounds

  1. Round 1: Idea Submission
  2. Round 2: Business Plan Presentation
  3. Round 3: Live Pitch Before Judges
  4. Final Round: Winner Announcement

Competition Rules

  • Participants can register individually or as a team.
  • Each team must present an original business idea.
  • Participants must explain their business model clearly.
  • Judges will evaluate creativity, feasibility and impact.
